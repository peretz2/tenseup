Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

TenseUp ("we", "our", or "the App") is an AI-powered English grammar practice application. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Voice & Text Data: When you use the speech-to-text feature, your spoken words are transcribed on your device using Apple's Speech framework or Whisper AI (Premium+ only). The transcribed text is sent to OpenAI's API for grammar analysis and AI conversation features.

Subscription Information: If you subscribe to TenseUp Premium or Premium+, payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment details. We receive only a confirmation of your subscription status through RevenueCat, our subscription management service.

Usage Data: We track basic usage counts (e.g., daily practice sessions, streak tracking) locally on your device using UserDefaults and SwiftData. This data is not transmitted to any external server.

Vocabulary Data: Words you save to your vocabulary list are stored locally on your device using SwiftData.

Third-Party AI Service - OpenAI

What data is sent to OpenAI:

  • Your spoken or typed messages during AI conversations
  • Your grammar practice exercises and answers
  • Role-play scenario conversations
  • Text you request to be analyzed or corrected
  • Conversation context to provide personalized responses

What data is NOT sent to OpenAI:

  • Your email address or Apple ID
  • Your payment information
  • Your device identifiers
  • Your saved vocabulary list
  • Your streak or usage statistics
  • Any personal profile information

Why we send data to OpenAI:
We use OpenAI's GPT models to provide AI-powered features including:

  • Natural conversation practice with AI
  • Real-time grammar corrections and explanations
  • Role-play scenario interactions
  • Personalized learning feedback

How OpenAI handles your data:
OpenAI processes your conversation data to generate AI responses. According to OpenAI's API Data Usage Policy, data submitted through their API:

  • Is not used to train OpenAI's models
  • May be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring
  • Is then deleted from OpenAI's systems

Your consent:
By using TenseUp's AI features (conversations, grammar check, role-play, lessons), you explicitly consent to your conversation data being sent to OpenAI for processing. You can stop using AI features at any time by closing the app or deleting your account.

Learn more about OpenAI's practices:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API Data Usage Policy

2. Other Third-Party Services

RevenueCat: Used to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID to verify subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your conversation data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Apple Speech Framework: Speech recognition (Standard mode) is processed on-device or through Apple's servers, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Whisper AI (Premium+ only): Advanced speech recognition is processed through OpenAI's Whisper API. Audio recordings are sent to OpenAI, transcribed, and then deleted. Transcriptions are handled as described in the OpenAI section above.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your practice history, vocabulary list, and usage statistics are stored locally on your device using SwiftData and UserDefaults. We do not operate external servers that store your personal data. All communication with third-party APIs (OpenAI, RevenueCat) uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

4. How to Control Your Data

Stop sharing data with OpenAI: Stop using the AI features (conversations, grammar check, role-play, lessons). The app will continue to work for vocabulary review and streak tracking.

Delete your local data: Delete the TenseUp app from your device. This will remove all locally stored practice history, vocabulary, and statistics.

Cancel subscription: Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

TenseUp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used our app, please contact us.

6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

If you are located in the European Union or California, you have certain rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data (stored locally on your device)
  • Deletion: Delete the app to remove all local data
  • Opt-out: Stop using AI features to stop sharing with OpenAI

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: Stored on your device until you delete the app
  • OpenAI data: Retained for up to 30 days, then deleted
  • RevenueCat data: Subscription status retained as long as you're subscribed

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
